### Audit and Logging

To ensure the security, integrity, and reliability of our platform, we maintain a detailed audit log of specific user activities. This section outlines what data is logged and how it is used.

**What data is logged?**

When you perform certain actions within the application, we may log the following information:

*   **User Information:** Your User ID and email address.
*   **Event Information:** The type of event that occurred (e.g., `user_login`, `create_test_case`, `delete_document`) and the timestamp of the event.
*   **Request Details:** The IP address from which the request was made and the User-Agent string of your browser or client.
*   **Event-Specific Context:** Additional details relevant to the event, such as the ID of a document you created, the filename of an uploaded file, or the rating you provided as feedback.

**Purpose of Logging**

We collect this data for the following legitimate business purposes:

*   **Security:** To monitor for and investigate suspicious or malicious activity.
*   **Auditing & Compliance:** To provide a trail of activities for compliance with internal policies and external regulations.
*   **Troubleshooting:** To diagnose and resolve technical issues with user accounts or the platform itself.
*   **Platform Analytics:** To generate anonymized, aggregate statistics about feature usage to improve our services.

**Who Can Access Log Data?**

Direct access to detailed, identifiable audit logs is strictly limited to authorized administrators. Access is granted only for the specific, legitimate purposes outlined above. All access to these audit logs by administrators is itself logged in a separate, secure system.

**Data Retention**

Audit log data is retained for a period necessary to meet our security and compliance obligations, after which it may be securely deleted or fully anonymized.
