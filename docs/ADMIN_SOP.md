# Administrator Standard Operating Procedure (SOP)

## Subject: Access to User Audit Data

### 1. Purpose

This document defines the rules and procedures for accessing the sensitive user audit data available in the "Admin Audit & Reporting" page. The purpose of these rules is to protect user privacy, ensure compliance with data protection regulations, and prevent misuse of administrative privileges.

### 2. Scope

This SOP applies to all personnel with administrator-level access to the AI Test Case Generator platform.

### 3. Rules of Engagement

Access to the detailed, identifiable user information in the "Audit View" is a privilege granted for specific, legitimate business purposes only. 

1.  **Permitted Use:** Access is permitted **only** for the following reasons:
    *   Investigating a security incident or suspected malicious activity.
    *   Responding to a direct user support request that requires examining their activity history.
    *   Fulfilling a formal request from a compliance or legal authority.

2.  **Prohibited Use:** Access for reasons of general curiosity, monitoring specific users without cause, or any other purpose not explicitly listed above is **strictly forbidden**.

3.  **Data Handling:** 
    *   Do not share, export, or screenshot data from the Audit View unless it is a required part of a documented security or compliance procedure.
    *   Do not discuss specific user activities or data with unauthorized personnel.

### 4. Monitoring and Enforcement

Be aware that all administrative access to the audit pages is itself logged in a separate, highly-secured "audit-the-auditors" log. This includes which administrator accessed the data, what filters they used, and when the access occurred.

Violation of this SOP will be treated as a serious security and policy infraction. Consequences may include, but are not limited to, immediate revocation of administrative privileges and further disciplinary action.

### 5. Acknowledgment

By accessing the Admin Audit & Reporting page, you acknowledge that you have read, understood, and agree to be bound by the terms of this SOP.
